The week, and the rule for bad weeks

Nine hours, same nine every week

Mon–Thu, pre-work45 min × 4 — one algorithm problem, start to finish
Saturday a.m.3 hrs — portfolio project, one meaningful commit
Sunday2 hrs — system design, then mocks from February
Floating evening1 hr — access track: messages, applications, reqs

A tenth hour goes to Saturday's project block, not to more problems.

Cut in this order

  1. Access track — one missed week of outreach costs almost nothing.
  2. Sunday's design block — design knowledge holds for a week.
  3. Saturday's project block — painful, but it has slack.
  4. Never the morning problem. Four 45-minute sessions is the floor.

A three-hour week is a legitimate week. Write it down and start the next one at full size.

The three non-negotiables

01One deployed service with real numbers — a URL, load-test figures, a README that reads like a design doc.
02120 problems solved, not watched. Solved means you wrote it, it passed, and you could do it again cold a month later.
03Thirty mock interviews out loud, with a human or at minimum a recording.
